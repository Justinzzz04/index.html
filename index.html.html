Focus Category: Entertainment

Television Energy Efficiency in Australia

Televisions account for a significant portion of home entertainment energy consumption. Display technologies (OLED vs. QLED vs. LED), screen sizes, and High Dynamic Range (HDR) modes dramatically affect power draw and electricity bills.

Compare TV Energy Profiles

Filter models by screen size and display tech in the Australian dataset

Standby Power & SDR vs HDR

  • Modern TVs consume under 0.5 Watts in standby mode in compliance with Australian MEPS regulations.
  • HDR (High Dynamic Range) content increases backlight brightness, raising power usage by up to 30% - 50% compared to standard SDR viewing.
  • Automatic Brightness Control (ABC) sensors adjust screen brightness based on ambient room light to optimize energy efficiency.

Screen Size Multiplier Effect

Moving from a 55-inch television to a 75-inch screen increases total display area by approximately 85%, requiring substantially more energy regardless of star rating.

Tip: Always check the annual kWh metric on the label, not just the star rating, when upgrading display dimensions.
